Policy · National Agenda

Our Founding
Agenda for Australia

The Sovereign National Party stands as a vanguard for Australia's autonomy, heritage and future. This agenda is our declaration to steer Australia toward a future where its sovereignty is inviolable and its citizens thrive — governance that cherishes and preserves public assets as national treasures, stewarded for the collective benefit of all Australians, now and into the future.

Core Ideologies

What We
Stand For

National Sovereignty
Australia's sovereign right to govern its own affairs — controlling our borders, making independent economic decisions, and preserving our cultural heritage — is non-negotiable.
Economic Prosperity
A dynamic open-market economy underpinned by prudent fiscal policies, minimal regulatory burdens, and strong support for entrepreneurship and free enterprise.
Social Cohesion
Unity and a shared sense of purpose strengthen our nation. We commit to a cohesive society that values respect, mutual understanding, and our collective heritage.
Responsible Governance
Integrity, transparency, and accountability are the cornerstones of the SNP. The interests of the Australian people are always — and unapologetically — at the forefront.
The Full Agenda

Ten Pillars of
the National Agenda

This is by no means an exhaustive list of our policy positions, but it lays the groundwork for a comprehensive strategy aimed at securing a prosperous future for all Australians.

01
Economic Growth & Family Support
Zero-interest family loans for homes and vehicles, charging only principal plus a fixed service fee. Tax incentives for first-time car owners and manufacturing incentives to drive demand across Australian industry.
02
Fair Tax, Real Reform
Abolition of personal income tax in favour of consumption-based taxation, paired with stricter corporate compliance to close loopholes and prevent avoidance.
03
National Manufacturing & Fair Imports
Fair duties on imported goods to prioritise Australian-made products, with revenue collected reinvested into public services and national development.
04
Sovereign Infrastructure & Resources
National energy, transport, water and mineral assets protected from foreign exploitation and retained under Australian ownership for the equitable benefit of all citizens.
05
Infrastructure Matched to Population
Housing, hospital, school and transport capacity set as the leading indicator for immigration intake, not the trailing one — no government grows the population faster than the services that support it.
06
Healthcare as a Right
An expanded state-funded hospital network on a non-profit basis, nominal co-payments, and heavily subsidised essential care guaranteed for citizens and Residents by Birth.
07
A Clear Citizenship Model
Citizens hold full and unquestioned rights. Residents build toward citizenship through military or civil service, sustained contribution, or a comprehensive citizenship test. Dual citizenship is not permitted.
08
Crime Reduction & Legal Reform
Youths aged 12 and over face legal accountability akin to adults, non-citizens convicted of crime face immediate deportation on sentencing, and rehabilitation divisions offer a structured path back for minor offenders.
09
Education Rebuilt Around Standards
Literacy, numeracy and science restored as core objectives, mandatory Australian history and civics, and vocational trade pathways funded on equal footing with university degrees.
10
National Defence & Service
Reinstated 18-month national service for Australian males aged 17 to 35, an honoured citizenship pathway through military service, and dedicated support centres for veterans and serving personnel.

Final Remarks

This document is by no means a complete or exhaustive list of our policy or agenda points. We recognise the dynamic nature of governance and remain open to innovation, adaptation, and the expansion of our policies to address the needs of Australia and its people effectively — leading Australia into a future where our sovereignty is unquestioned, our economy flourishes, and our society is unified by shared values and aspirations.
