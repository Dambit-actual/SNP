Policy · Australian Labour Corps

A Permanent National
Civilian Workforce

The Australian Labour Corps is a permanent, national, civilian institution designed to restore Australia's ability to build, maintain and repair the physical foundations of the nation efficiently and affordably. It is not welfare, not nationalisation, and not militarisation — it is a disciplined civilian workforce that integrates paid training and real-world delivery into a single system, funded by redirecting existing public works, housing and training budgets rather than new taxes.

The Framework

How the Corps
Is Built to Work

Two Operational Divisions
The Public Works and Heavy Infrastructure Division delivers highways, bridges, dams and flood mitigation. The Community and Residential Works Division provides fixed-price repairs, maintenance and housing at scale for ordinary households.
Paid From Day One
Full-time employment on standard Monday-to-Friday hours, with no unpaid overtime culture. Pay progresses through Starter, Skilled, Senior and Master tiers based on both competency and time.
Training Integrated With Real Work
Every member trains on live worksites under paid mentors, fully aligned with existing national trade qualifications. No parallel certification and no prolonged classroom-only delay before producing.
A Bond With No Debt Traps
A limited bond recovers only the cost of tools, PPE and uniforms, never wages or training, and extinguishes naturally within six to twelve months or on qualification. Members may leave at any time.
Union-Free, Trade-Style Discipline
A direct employment relationship built on trust, leadership development and clear grievance pathways. Retraining comes before punishment, and leadership failure is held to a higher standard than trainee error.
A Real Pathway to Citizenship
Service in the Labour Corps is a recognised civil service pathway to citizenship for eligible residents, consistent with the SNP's broader citizenship framework.
A Structured Path to Rehabilitation
Dedicated divisions provide eligible offenders with discipline, trade skills and a genuine pathway to reintegration, alongside the Corps' broader national service and emergency reconstruction divisions.
Backing Australian Manufacturing
Procurement is directed to Australian-made tools, vehicles, plant and equipment wherever appropriate, turning the Corps into a stable demand anchor for domestic industry.

The Bottom Line

A structural correction to how Australia builds its future, not a temporary program or ideological experiment — converting fragmented spending into owned national capability, delivering more infrastructure, more housing, more skills and greater economic resilience for the same public investment.
