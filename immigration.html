Policy · Immigration

A Fair, Controlled
and Sustainable System

Our immigration policy is built on national sovereignty, social cohesion and responsible governance. We control our own borders, we set immigration settings that serve the national interest, and we expect newcomers to integrate into Australian life, contribute meaningfully and respect the values that hold our society together. Immigration to Australia is a privilege, not a right, and we intend to keep it that way.

The Framework

Eight Pillars of
Our Immigration Policy

Secure Borders, Controlled Entry
Dedicated patrol units, drone and AI-driven surveillance, and naval intercepts for unauthorised arrivals. Merit-based visas across visitor, work and skills, and humanitarian categories. Overstays and breaches carry serious consequences, including a minimum ten-year re-entry ban.
Skilled Visas That Work
Every Work and Skills Visa requires a signed job contract before arrival, a sponsoring employer, and proof no local worker was available. Visa holders stay in their nominated occupation for the life of the visa. Real worker protections: direct wage payment, criminal penalties for passport confiscation, and the right to leave a breaching sponsor.
Australian Values, Australian Law
Everyone who calls Australia home is expected to respect our Christian cultural heritage, live under Australian law alone, and take part in the life of the community. No parallel legal systems or cultural enclaves apart from the rest of the nation.
Residency Built on Contribution
Non-citizens who come to live and work here are classified as Residents, free to build a life in Australia but without access to voting, public office, government healthcare, public schooling or other taxpayer-funded services, reserved for citizens.
A Real Pathway to Citizenship
After a minimum of seven years' residency, pathways open through military or civil service, a comprehensive citizenship test, sustained economic contribution, or other demonstrated contributions to national life. Partners of citizens get a faster track, not a free pass.
Growth Matched to Infrastructure
Annual intake set against real capacity: housing supply, hospital beds and GP access, school places, transport load and utility headroom, reviewed every year. Population growth will not be allowed to outrun the services Australians rely on.
Australian Homes for Australians
Immediate revocation of provisions letting international students and other temporary visa holders buy residential property, closing the loophole for overseas family members too, with a review of purchases already made under the old rules.
Zero Tolerance for Crime
Non-citizens convicted of a crime, including visa holders, face immediate deportation on sentencing, for themselves and their family. Minor offences by residents may be addressed through mandated national service, subject to independent oversight.
The Bottom Line

A secure, self-reliant Australia where immigration strengthens the nation rather than straining it. Controlled borders, genuine contribution, infrastructure that keeps pace, and a shared national identity — that's what a responsible immigration system looks like.

Authorised by J. Stevens, Gold Coast, Qld.
